# Demo Video Script (about 60–90 seconds)

Use an emulator or a connected phone and record the screen. Keep the recording short and show the actual app.

1. **Login (10–15 sec):** launch the app, enter `demo@example.com` and any non-empty password, tap **Sign in**.
2. **Browse products (15 sec):** show the product grid and product images/prices.
3. **Add to cart (10 sec):** add two products; add one of them twice to demonstrate quantity merging and the cart badge.
4. **Cart (20–25 sec):** open the Cart tab, show the products and total, increase quantity, decrease quantity, then remove one product.
5. **State retention (5–10 sec):** switch to Shop and back to Cart; show that the remaining cart contents are still there.
6. **Close (5 sec):** briefly show the finished cart state.

Suggested filename: `demo.mp4`. If the repository size limit is a concern, upload the video to Google Drive or an unlisted video link and add the link to the README.
